The Itinerary

Five Days of Devotion

September 6, 6:30 PM

Aagman Yatra

The grand procession begins from the Central Arcade towards the Hostel Block (Panchaangan). Join us in welcoming Bappa with Dhol beats.

September 7, 11:00 AM

Sthapana & Maha Aarti

Sthapana Pooja commences at the New Panchaangan. The first Maha Aarti of the festival will take place in the evening at 7:30 PM.

September 8, 9:00 PM

Cultural Night

A mesmerizing evening featuring Kathak performances, flute recitals, and an open mic exclusively for the IITGN community.

September 11, 5:00 PM

Visarjan

The final Maha Aarti, followed immediately by the grand Visarjan procession moving campus-wide from the New Panchaangan.
