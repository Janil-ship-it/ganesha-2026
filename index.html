The Grand Itinerary

Five days of non-stop devotion, music, and celebration.

September 6

Aagman Yatra

The grand procession begins from the Central Arcade towards the Hostel Block (Panchaangan) at 6:30 PM. Join us in welcoming Bappa with Dhol beats.

September 7

Sthapana & Maha Aarti

Sthapana Pooja commences at 11:00 AM at the New Panchaangan. The first Maha Aarti of the festival will take place at 7:30 PM.

September 8

Cultural Night & Open Mic

A mesmerizing evening starting at 9:00 PM featuring Kathak performances, flute recitals, and an open mic for the IITGN community.

September 11

Maha Aarti & Visarjan

The final Maha Aarti at 5:00 PM, followed by the grand Visarjan procession at 6:00 PM starting from the New Panchaangan.
